What the extension checks

Content without JavaScript

Most AI crawlers read the raw HTML and skip scripts. The extension compares the raw page with what you see and shows the share of text bots miss.

Structure that gets quoted

Question headings, short answers under them, lists, tables, numbers and sources. 17 checks add up to one score from 0 to 100.

Access for AI bots

Which AI crawlers your robots.txt allows or blocks, whether you have llms.txt, and any noindex or noai tags.

How to use it

  1. Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Open any page and click the extension icon. The check takes about 3 seconds.
  3. Read your score and the top problems, then fix them and check again.

Everything runs in your browser. Page content is never sent to our servers or to any AI model.

Questions

Does a high score guarantee my page will appear in ChatGPT answers?

No. Nobody outside the AI companies knows exactly how sources are chosen. The score measures how easy your page is to read and quote, based on patterns that help in practice.

Do you send my pages anywhere?

No. The extension downloads and analyzes pages inside your browser. The only external request is a check of your Pro purchase status.

Can it see if Cloudflare blocks AI bots on my site?

Not reliably. Requests come from your own browser and IP address, so firewall rules that target bots by IP do not apply to them.
