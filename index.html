How do AI crawlers see your page?

Most AI crawlers download your HTML and do not run JavaScript. Anything your site builds in the browser, like tabs, accordions, review widgets and tables loaded from an API, may never reach ChatGPT or Perplexity.

Rawview fetches the raw HTML of the page you are on, compares it with what Chrome rendered, and marks every block that AI crawlers miss.

How do I check my AI visibility?

  1. Add Rawview to Chrome.
  2. Open the page you want to check and click the Rawview icon.
  3. Read your GEO score and the top problems. The check takes about 3 seconds.
  4. Fix the problems and run the check again to see the new score.

Pages are analyzed in your browser and never sent to a server or an AI model.

Questions about AI visibility

What is an AI visibility checker?

An AI visibility checker shows whether AI search tools like ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity can read your page and how easy it is for them to quote. Rawview does this inside Chrome for any page you open.

What is a GEO score?

GEO stands for generative engine optimization. Rawview's GEO score runs 17 checks on structure, facts, trust signals and crawler access, and adds them up to a number from 0 to 100.

Does a high GEO score guarantee citations in ChatGPT?

No. AI companies do not publish how they choose sources. The score measures how easy a page is to read and quote, which is what you can control.

Does Rawview send my pages anywhere?

No. All analysis runs in your browser. The only outside request is a check of your Pro purchase.

Can Rawview see if Cloudflare blocks AI bots?

Not reliably. Requests come from your browser and IP address, so firewall rules that target bot IP ranges do not apply to them.
