Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

RawView is built to work without collecting your data. This policy explains what little information is involved. It is provided by Dariia Zhurakivska, Ukraine.

Pages you check

The extension reads the page in your current tab, and downloads that page's raw HTML, robots.txt and llms.txt, plus any competitor URL you enter. All analysis happens inside your browser. Page content, URLs and scores are never sent to us or to any third party, and no AI model is used.

Data stored on your device

The extension saves your settings, your Pro status and (in Pro) your last 50 checks with their URLs and scores in Chrome's local storage. This data stays on your device. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

Purchases

When you buy Pro, Paddle collects your name, email, country and payment details as our Merchant of Record. We receive your email and order details so we can activate Pro and answer support requests. We never see your full card details. Paddle's own privacy policy applies to the data it processes.

To check your Pro status, the extension sends your purchase email to Paddle or to our license check service. Nothing else is sent with it.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses fonts from Google Fonts. GitHub and Google may log your IP address for security and operations. We use no analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

What we do not do

We do not sell data, share it for advertising or use it to train AI models. The extension's use of data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing rawview.app@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. Tax and payment records may have to be kept for the period required by law.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
